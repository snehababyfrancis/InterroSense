AI-Assisted Interrogation System

Integrating Qwen1.5-1.8B-Chat LLM with real-time micro-expression, gaze, and head pose analysis to enhance accuracy and consistency in suspect evaluation. Reducing human bias and operational fatigue through adaptive, scenario-driven interrogation.

System Capabilities

The AI-Assisted Interrogation System integrates multimodal evidence analysis to provide law enforcement with scalable, adaptive, and transparent interrogation support.

LLM Integration

Fine-tuned Qwen1.5-1.8B-Chat model conducts adaptive, chat-based interrogations, modifying questioning strategy based on suspect responses.

Visual Analysis

Real-time micro-expression, gaze, and head pose detection through computer vision models to identify non-verbal deception indicators.

Deception Probability

Fuses multimodal evidence to produce real-time deception probability scores with interpretable explanations for the officer.

Bias Reduction

Trained on deception dataset and custom scenario-rich corpus to reduce human bias and operational fatigue in interrogations.
